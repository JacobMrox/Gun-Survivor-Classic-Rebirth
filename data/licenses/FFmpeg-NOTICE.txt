FFmpeg shared runtime libraries

This package includes x86 builds of libavcodec 61, libavformat 61,
libavutil 59, libswscale 8 and libswresample 5. They are required by
Classic Rebirth's movie playback code. Keep all five DLLs together
beside d3d8.dll and gun_bio.exe.

These libraries identify their license as GNU Lesser General Public
License version 2.1 or later. The license text is in FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt.

The repository includes the FFmpeg source and vcpkg build material under:
source/vcpkg_installed/x86-windows/vcpkg/blds/ffmpeg/
The upstream project is https://ffmpeg.org/ .

These MSVC builds also require the Microsoft Visual C++ x86 runtime.
FFmpeg-runtime.sha256 records the hashes of the supplied DLLs.
